# Early-warning study (extension B)

Generated by `python scripts/early_warning_study.py`. Same data, same 1712 test days
(2020-01-02 to 2026-10-23), same ACI step size and floor.

**The signal.** A logistic regression fitted on the training years only, from eight inputs known at
the close of day t (short- against long-run volatility, drawdown, recent jumps, cross-asset
correlation, the VIX, its change, and its premium over the model's own volatility forecast). It
predicts whether any of the next five days breaches a Gaussian 99% VaR; that happened in
14% of training windows. **AUC 0.68 on the calibration years
and 0.62 on the test years** (0.5 is a coin flip).

**How it is used.** Days are sorted into three regimes by terciles of the signal (thresholds from
the calibration years), and ACI runs separately inside each regime. The control sorts days by
current volatility instead, so any gain from the learned signal has to beat a simple regime label.

|-------------------------------------|---------------|------------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------|
| ACI, one regime                     | 4.91%         | 0.859      | 0.072              | 21%                                          | 16                                        | 1.37%      |
| ACI by current volatility (control) | 5.02%         | 0.965      | 0.000              | 30%                                          | 16                                        | 1.38%      |
| ACI by learned stress probability   | 4.79%         | 0.688      | 0.019              | 26%                                          | 14                                        | 1.41%      |
| Filtered historical simulation      | 5.26%         | 0.628      | 0.001              | 30%                                          | 18                                        | 1.30%      |
### 99% VaR (target breach rate 1%)

| Method                              | Breach rate   | Kupiec p   | Christoffersen p   | Breaches followed by another within 5 days   | Breaches in first 5 days of 15 episodes   | Mean VaR   |
|-------------------------------------|---------------|------------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------|
| ACI, one regime                     | 0.93%         | 0.783      | 0.008              | 12%                                          | 10                                        | 3.14%      |
| ACI by current volatility (control) | 0.99%         | 0.977      | 0.010              | 12%                                          | 10                                        | 2.71%      |
| ACI by learned stress probability   | 0.93%         | 0.783      | 0.008              | 12%                                          | 9                                         | 2.82%      |
| Filtered historical simulation      | 1.05%         | 0.832      | 0.182              | 6%                                           | 9                                         | 2.21%      |

*Episodes* are 15 stress onsets, defined from returns alone before any method was
compared: the first day the 5-day RMS return exceeds twice its training-period median after 21
calm days; the window is the five days ending on that day (75 days in all). With so few
episodes, differences of one or two breaches are noise. *Mean VaR* is the average size of the loss
limit, a proxy for the capital the method ties up.
